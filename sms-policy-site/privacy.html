NeuroProxy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

NeuroProxy is a prototype that helps users organize conversation context and review messages before sending. For questions about this policy, contact dingonewen@gmail.com.

Information we use

The prototype may process contact names and phone numbers, conversation text, message drafts and replies, and voice input. Conversation and contact data are stored in the application's local database. Browser speech recognition may send audio to the browser's speech provider.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to provide conversation analysis, message drafting, delivery, and context recovery. Relevant text may be sent to the configured AI provider; call audio may be processed by ElevenLabs; and phone numbers and messages are sent to Photon for communications. These services process information to provide their services.

SMS consent and phone numbers are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS choices

SMS recipients may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. See the SMS Terms & Conditions.

Access and deletion

Contact us at the address above with privacy or deletion requests. Users can delete saved conversations from the application; messages already delivered to recipients and data retained by service providers may not be deleted by that action.
