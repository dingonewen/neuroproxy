SMS Terms & Conditions

Last updated: November 14, 2026

NeuroProxy is an experimental communication and accessibility tool. It can help users draft and send individual, user-approved messages to contacts who have agreed to receive them.

Message frequency and costs

Message frequency varies based on use. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact dingonewen@gmail.com. Opt-out requests must be honored.

Consent and privacy

NeuroProxy users must obtain a recipient's consent before sending. See the Privacy Policy for information about phone numbers, messaging consent, and service providers.
